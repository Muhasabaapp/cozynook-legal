Privacy Policy for CozyNook

Last updated: October 7, 2026

CozyNook ("the app") is developed by Fazliddin Ramatillayev ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Summary

CozyNook does not have its own servers and does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The habits, coins, and room you build inside the app are stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, on your device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage:

  • The habits you create and your completion history
  • Your coin balance and the furniture you have purchased or placed
  • Your streak and streak-freeze history
  • Your notification preference (on/off)

None of this information is sent to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, this data is deleted from your device.

Subscriptions and payment

CozyNook offers an optional Pro subscription, sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never see or receive your payment details, card number, or Apple ID password. Apple's own privacy policy governs how Apple handles your purchase: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device using Apple's notification system. No notification content or schedule is sent to us.

Sharing a room image

If you choose to share a picture of your room (for example to Instagram or Messages), that image is generated on your device and shared only through the share options you select. We do not receive a copy of it.

Children's privacy

CozyNook is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Analytics and advertising

The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
